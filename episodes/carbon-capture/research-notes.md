# 🌱 Carbon Capture — Podcast Research Notes

## Episode Theme Overview
Carbon dioxide removal (CDR) technologies aim to pull CO₂ directly from the atmosphere or enhance natural carbon sinks. This branch covers open-source tools and models for carbon capture, utilization, and storage.

---

## 🔬 Key Projects Discovered

### 1. ClimateMARGO.jl (⭐ 73 | Julia | MIT)
- **Repo:** [ClimateMARGO/ClimateMARGO.jl](https://github.com/ClimateMARGO/ClimateMARGO.jl)
- **Description:** Julia implementation of MARGO — includes carbon dioxide removal optimization alongside solar geoengineering.
- **Stars:** 73 | **Forks:** 13
- **License:** MIT
- **Relevance:** Models the trade-offs between CDR and SRM strategies — essential for understanding the full geoengineering portfolio.

### 2. CDR Database by CarbonPlan (⭐ 29 | Python)
- **Repo:** [carbonplan/cdr-database](https://github.com/carbonplan/cdr-database)
- **Description:** Open database tracking carbon removal projects — evaluates CDR effectiveness and permanence.
- **Stars:** 29
- **Last Updated:** Ongoing (1-2 commits/month)
- **ReadMe Highlights:**
  - Systematic evaluation of carbon removal ventures
  - Focus on measurability, additionality, and permanence
  - Used by policymakers and researchers to assess CDR credibility
- **Trend Analysis:** Steady low-activity maintenance mode. The scientific work is complete; the project is now in the policy uptake phase.

### 3. CarbonPlan Forest Risks (⭐ 35 | Python)
- **Repo:** [carbonplan/forest-risks](https://github.com/carbonplan/forest-risks)
- **Description:** Statistical models for forest carbon risk — evaluates the reliability of forest-based carbon credits.
- **Stars:** 35
- **Last Updated:** Dormant
- **Trend Analysis:** Research phase complete. Results published and now influencing carbon market regulation.

### 4. Carbon Swarm (⭐ 2 | Python)
- **Repo:** [swarmlabsusa/carbon-swarm](https://github.com/swarmlabsusa/carbon-swarm)
- **Description:** Deterministic carbon capture & conversion intelligence — maps technologies, economics, scalability for CO₂ removal.
- **Stars:** 2
- **Last Updated:** 2026-04-13
- **Relevance:** Newest entrant, mapping the CDR technology landscape.

### 5. CCU-LCA bymassimopizzol (⭐ 14 | Jupyter Notebook)
- **Repo:** [massimopizzol/CCU-LCA](https://github.com/massimopizzol/CCU-LCA)
- **Description:** Consequential life cycle assessment of carbon capture and utilization technologies within the chemical industry.
- **Stars:** 14
- **Last Updated:** 2026-06-23
- **Relevance:** Rigorous academic LCA analysis — great for the "are carbon capture claims legitimate?" episode angle.

### 6. CCUS Maptool by SciGaP (⭐ 8 | JetBrains MPS)
- **Repo:** [SciGaP/simccs-maptool](https://github.com/SciGaP/simccs-maptool)
- **Description:** Online decision-support tool for evaluating carbon capture, utilization, and storage technologies (CCUS).
- **Stars:** 8
- **Last Updated:** 2026-04-10
- **Relevance:** Practical tool for policymakers and industry stakeholders.
